Get started

Your company is recognised by your work email's domain. Create it if you're the first one here, or join it if someone already has.

Create your company

Choose your company's address, such as acme.coremode.ai, and see straight away whether it's free. Then sign in with your work Google account to claim it. You become its administrator.

Join your company

If your company is already on coremode, sign in with your work Google account and you'll join it. An administrator there gives you a seat.

Sign in

coremode is opening to companies a few at a time. If yours is not open yet, we hold the address you chose and write to you when it is.
