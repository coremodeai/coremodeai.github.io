Built so that it cannot become surveillance

A system that reads a company's work could easily turn into a way of watching people. We designed against that from the first line of code, as structure and not as a setting someone can change.

Three kinds of knowledge

Knowledge flows to you, never up from you

Everything coremode knows belongs to one of three places. What is shared can inform what is yours. What is yours never informs what is shared.

Your organization

Shared with everyone in the company

Company goals, who reports to whom, announcements, and work done in shared channels and tools that everyone can already see.

A private space

Shared with its members only

A leadership group, a hiring loop, a deal. Only its members can see what is in it. To everyone else, it does not exist: no name, no member list, no count.

You

Yours alone

Your email, your direct messages, your notes, and your personal objectives. They shape what coremode shows you and no one else.

What that means in practice

What is private stays private
Your email, your direct messages, and your notes inform only what coremode shows you. They never reach your manager, your company's administrators, or anyone else.
Seniority opens nothing
A chief executive or an administrator sees a private space only if they are a member of it. Otherwise they cannot tell that it exists.
The system decides access, not the model
Who may see what is enforced by ordinary, tested code. An AI model is never asked whether to show something to someone.
Everything traces to its source
Each thing coremode tells you links to the messages and documents behind it, in the tools they came from, so you can check it.
What you conclude stays as private as what you read
A summary, a count, or a signal built from private material is exactly as private as that material. Turning something into a number does not make it shareable.
Losing access takes effect at once
When someone leaves a space or the company, what they could see because of it stops being shown to them straight away, not at the next scheduled update.

What your employer can and cannot see

coremode is there to support the people doing the work. It is not a tool for measuring them.

Can see

  • Company goals and the progress toward them
  • Who is in the company and who reports to whom
  • What happens in shared channels and tools, as they already can
  • The private spaces they are themselves a member of

Cannot see

  • Your email, direct messages, or notes
  • What coremode shows you in your Focus or Perspective
  • Private spaces they are not a member of, or that those exist
  • Scores, rankings, or activity counts for individual people; coremode does not produce them

What we do with your company's data

It is used to run coremode for you
Your company's data produces your company's signals. One company's data is never used for another's.
It does not train shared models
We do not train models on your data to benefit other customers. If that ever becomes worth offering, it will be something you choose, not something you find out about.
Our own staff are kept out of the content
The tools we use to run the service manage accounts, not what is in them.
The same on every plan
These protections are not a paid extra. They are the same on Starter, Business, and Enterprise.
