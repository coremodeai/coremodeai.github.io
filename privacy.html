Built so it cannot become surveillance.

By structure, not by a setting.

Knowledge flows to you, never up from you

What is shared can inform what is yours. Never the reverse.

Shared with everyone

Your organization

Company goals, reporting lines, and work in shared channels.

Members only

A private space

A leadership group, a hiring loop, a deal. To everyone else, it does not exist.

Yours alone

You

Your email, direct messages, and notes. They shape what you see, and no one else's.

In practice

Seniority opens nothing

A chief executive sees a private space only as a member.

Code decides access

Ordinary, tested code. A model is never asked who may see what.

Access ends at once

Leave a space or the company, and what you saw through it is gone.

Summaries stay private

A count built from private material is as private as the material.

Everything has a source

What coremode tells you links to the messages and documents behind it.

No scores

No rankings or activity counts for individual people. coremode does not produce them.

What your employer sees

coremode supports people. It does not measure them.

Can see

  • Company goals and progress toward them
  • Who is in the company, and who reports to whom
  • Shared channels and tools, as they already can
  • Private spaces they are a member of

Cannot see

  • Your email, direct messages, or notes
  • Your Focus or your Perspective
  • Private spaces they are not in, or that those exist
  • Scores for individual people

Your company's data

Yours only

Your data produces your signals. Never another company's.

No shared training

We do not train models on your data for other customers.

Staff stay out

Our tools manage accounts, not what is in them.

Every plan

The same protections on Starter, Business, and Enterprise.

coremode is in early development. This is the design; ask us what is in place today. A formal privacy policy comes before general availability.
