How it works

Someone mentions in a busy channel that a date slipped.

  1. 1

    Read once

    The meaning is kept: who said what, what was promised, what changed.

  2. 2

    Matched

    To a commitment, a goal, and an owner. If unclear, it stays unmatched. No guessing.

  3. 3

    Worked out

    What depends on the new date, and who expects the old one. Software computes it, never a model.

  4. 4

    Delivered

    The owner gets it in Focus. Others who need to know get a signal. Everyone else hears nothing.
