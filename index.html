How it works, in detail

Built so that it cannot become surveillance

A system that reads a company's work could easily turn into a way of watching people. We designed against that from the first line of code, as structure and not as a setting.

What is private stays private
Your email, your direct messages, and your notes inform only what coremode shows you. They never reach your manager, your company's administrators, or anyone else.
Seniority opens nothing
A chief executive or an administrator sees a private space only if they are a member of it. Otherwise they cannot tell that it exists.
The system decides access, not the model
Who may see what is enforced by ordinary, tested code. An AI model is never asked whether to show something to someone.
Everything traces to its source
Each thing coremode tells you links to the messages and documents behind it, in the tools they came from, so you can check it.

It works alongside the tools you already use

coremode does not replace your chat, your email, or your project tracker. It reads them, and when something needs doing it does it there: sends the email, posts the message, creates the issue.

Starting with Google Workspace and Slack. Linear, Okta, and Workday follow.
