Pricing

Priced per person, per month. Every plan has the same privacy protections; we do not charge extra for keeping private things private.

coremode is in early development. These are the prices we plan to launch with, and design partners agree their terms with us directly.

Starter

For a small team finding out what coremode does for them.

$9 per person, per month

  • Up to 10 people
  • Focus, Signals, and Perspective for everyone
  • Google Workspace and Slack
  • A personal space for each person, and 2 private spaces
  • Actions run only when you approve them
  • Set up by yourself, without a call

Business

For a company where coordination across teams has started to cost real time.

$49 per person, per month

  • Up to 50 people
  • Everything in Starter, with 10 private spaces
  • Objectives that run from company goals down to each person
  • Alignment across teams
  • More connections, including Linear and your HR system
  • Sign-in through your identity provider
  • Actions that run by themselves under rules you set

Questions people ask

Who counts as a person?
Anyone in your company who has coremode. You pay for the people using it, not for everyone in your directory.
Is privacy different between plans?
No. Private email and messages stay private, and seniority opens nothing, on every plan.
Can we start small and move up?
Yes. Moving from Starter to Business keeps everything coremode has already learned about your company.
Can we use it today?
You can reserve your company's address today. We are setting companies up a few at a time while the product is in early development, starting with design partners.

See it with your own company's work

A demo takes half an hour. We show you Focus, Signals, and Perspective, and talk through what connecting your tools would involve. We are working with a small number of companies of 30 to 150 people while the product is in early development.
