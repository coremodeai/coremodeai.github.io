How it works

coremode connects to the tools your company already works in. Behind it is one engine with three parts, and everything you see comes from them.

The Coremode Engine

It works without being asked

Most AI tools wait for a question. The engine runs in the background all day, keeps track of where your company is trying to go, and comes to the right person when something needs them.

  1. Objectives

    The destination

    You say what the company is trying to achieve. Goals run from the company down to each team and person, so the engine knows what matters, and to whom.

  2. Knowledge

    The map

    The engine reads your tools continuously and keeps a current picture of who is doing what, what was promised, and what changed. Nobody writes a status update.

  3. Signals

    The moment you are needed

    When something bears on an objective, the engine tells the person it concerns: a decision to make, an action ready to go, or simply something worth knowing.

What happens to a message

Take one message in a busy channel: someone mentions that a date has slipped. This is what coremode does with it.

  1. It is read once

    The meaning is taken out and kept: who said what, what was promised, what changed. The message is not read again each time someone opens the app.

  2. It is matched to what the company already knows

    Which commitment is this about, which goal does that commitment serve, and who owns it? When that is unclear, coremode leaves it unmatched instead of guessing.

  3. The consequences are worked out

    What now depends on the new date, and who is expecting the old one. Counts and dates are computed by ordinary software, never estimated by a model.

  4. It reaches the people it concerns, and no one else

    The owner gets something to act on in Focus. People who only need to know get a signal. Everyone else hears nothing, because nothing changed for them.

What you can do from a card

Finish it
Each kind of card has its own buttons. A decision offers its options. An action offers to run what was prepared, such as sending the drafted email. An alignment lets you state which side you are on, where the others involved can see it.
Set it aside until something
"Later" always asks when it should come back: a time, or an event it is waiting on. It returns by itself. After two postponements it asks you to hand it off or drop it.
Check the evidence
Every card lists the messages, documents, and issues it is based on, and opens them in the tool they came from.
Add what you know
A comment becomes part of your organization's knowledge. If it matters to someone else, it reaches them as a signal.

It works alongside the tools you already use

coremode does not replace your chat, your email, or your project tracker. It reads them, and when something needs doing it does it there: sends the email, posts the message, creates the issue.

Starting with Google Workspace and Slack. Linear, Okta, and Workday follow.
